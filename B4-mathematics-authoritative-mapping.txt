OFFICIAL CONTENT STANDARD

B4.2.1.1

Demonstrate an understanding of how to identify and describe
patterns found in tables and charts, including a multiplication chart.


OFFICIAL LEARNING INDICATORS

B4.2.1.1.1
Describe the pattern found in a given table or chart.

B4.2.1.1.2
Determine the missing element(s) in a given table or chart.

B4.2.1.1.3
Identify the error(s) in a given table or chart.


OFFICIAL CONTENT STANDARD

B4.2.1.3

Represent, describe and extend patterns and relationships,
using charts and tables, to solve problems.


OFFICIAL LEARNING INDICATORS

B4.2.1.3.1
Translate the information in a given problem into a table or chart.

B4.2.1.3.2
Identify and extend the patterns in a table or chart to solve
a given problem.

MAPPING PRINCIPLE

FILUMIRE competencies are adaptive learning units and are not identical
to NaCCA curriculum indicators.

A FILUMIRE competency may support more than one official curriculum node,
and one official curriculum node may eventually map to several FILUMIRE
competencies.

Existing FILUMIRE competency IDs and learner evidence/history remain unchanged.

This mapping establishes authoritative curriculum alignment for these
specific competencies only. It does not imply that the remainder of the
B4 Mathematics curriculum has yet been implemented in FILUMIRE.
