## Controlled-pilot gate

- Every objective in the declared pilot scope has an adjudicated source association and adequate approved teaching/assessment; exclusions are explicit. No missing or pending record is counted as complete.
- Productive skills have sufficient independently produced evidence through existing supported systems. Guided retries remain guided; observations and teacher judgements are genuine.
- EN/FR educational approval is recorded by the actual reviewers. Original-language retained questions are distinguished from translation fallback.
- Both-language correct, incorrect, repeated-error, retention and transfer paths pass with stable assessment/evidence identity, interruptions, resumes and progress reporting.
- The current source checks, 40 selected suites, TypeScript, production build and 28 browser cases continue to pass. Extend competency-specific execution beyond the earlier 12-topic sample; do not equate sample tests with execution of every topic.
- Isolated database tests prove durable evidence, idempotent reconnect, restart/resume and due revision. Real phones and classroom review verify accessibility and low-connectivity usability. Full offline adaptive learning remains a separate scope decision; the current shell queues one answer and displays an offline fallback.
